The National Assembly Standing Committee on Parliamentary Affairs met at Parliament House in Islamabad to conduct formal consultations regarding ongoing electoral administration processes.

Officials from the Election Commission of Pakistan presented an operational briefing outlining current logistical readiness, digital register updates, and inter-agency coordination mechanisms. Committee members reviewed regulatory submissions and scheduled further follow-up sessions.
