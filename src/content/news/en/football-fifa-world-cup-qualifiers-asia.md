The Asian Football Confederation (AFC) qualification campaign for the 2026 FIFA World Cup has produced a tightly contested round of fixtures across regional host cities, according to official match records released by FIFA.

With eight direct Asian qualifying slots available under the tournament's expanded 48-nation format, traditional continental powerhouses and emerging sides engaged in intense technical battles. Matchday results saw vital points secured in defensive stalemates, preserving narrow margins at the summit of the groups.

Match officials commended stadium attendance figures and the implementation of semi-automated offside technology across all continental venues. Participating member federations now prepare for the critical subsequent window scheduled for next month.
