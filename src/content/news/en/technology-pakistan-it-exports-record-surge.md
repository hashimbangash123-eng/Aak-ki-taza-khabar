Pakistan's information technology and IT-enabled services (ITeS) export remittances experienced double-digit growth during the recent fiscal quarter, according to verified performance metrics published by the Ministry of Information Technology and the State Bank of Pakistan.

Official trade ledger data shows strong foreign exchange earnings across enterprise software development, artificial intelligence workflows, and offshore support contracts. The growth has been stimulated by simplified banking mechanisms allowing tech enterprises to retain up to fifty percent of their foreign export revenues in dedicated specialized foreign currency accounts.

Industry associations including PASHA confirmed that international contracts with North American, Gulf, and European clients expanded steadily over the period. Telecom authorities also credited accelerated national broadband rollouts and specialized software technology park incubators for facilitating freelance and start-up expansion across secondary urban centers.

Officials emphasized that structural priorities for the remainder of the fiscal year include scaling vocational certification programs for young software developers and expanding digital payment gateways.
