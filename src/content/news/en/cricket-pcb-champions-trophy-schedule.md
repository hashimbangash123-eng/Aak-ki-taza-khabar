The Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) has released an infrastructure progress briefing confirming key milestones in the comprehensive renovation of premier cricket stadiums in Lahore, Karachi, and Rawalpindi.

According to technical specifications published by the board's project management wing, the major civil construction phases at Gaddafi Stadium Lahore are progressing on schedule. The upgrades include newly erected hospitality boxes, high-definition digital display systems, and modernized player dressing rooms meeting international athletic standards.

Simultaneously, work at the National Stadium Karachi has focused on improving sightlines for spectators, upgrading floodlight towers to LED specifications, and expanding broadcast facilities to accommodate ultra-high-definition international coverage.

The PCB confirmed that technical representatives from international cricket governing bodies have conducted initial walk-through assessments, reviewing access routes, security protocols, and pitch maintenance setups. The board stated that finishing works will conclude ahead of test event fixtures.
