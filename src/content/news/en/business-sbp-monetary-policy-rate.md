The Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) of the State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) concluded its scheduled rate review meeting in Karachi, announcing the decision on the national benchmark policy rate.

In its official monetary policy statement released following the session, the committee noted that headline inflation has continued its downward trajectory over recent months, supported by fiscal discipline and stable commodity prices. However, the committee emphasized that external financing risks and forthcoming debt repayment schedules necessitate a prudent macroeconomic stance.

According to data presented by the central bank, foreign exchange reserves held by the SBP have stabilized, supported by recent multilateral disbursements and steady home remittances from overseas Pakistani workers. The MPC reiterated that maintaining price stability remains its primary statutory objective, with medium-term inflation targeted within the five to seven percent range.

Financial market analysts noted that the decision reflects a measured balance between stimulating industrial credit growth and preventing sudden volatility in the foreign exchange market. The next monetary policy meeting is scheduled to evaluate updated fiscal quarter figures.
