The Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan has officially announced the launch of a new cycle of national research fellowships aimed at strengthening indigenous research capabilities in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM).

Under the programmatic framework unveiled by the commission, postgraduate scholars enrolled in accredited Pakistani public universities will receive direct grant allocations covering laboratory materials, access to specialized scientific computing equipment, and publication subsidies in international indexed journals.

HEC officials underscored that the priority disciplines for the funding cycle include applied biotechnology, data science, renewable energy systems, and advanced agricultural genomics. The initiative also reserves targeted funding allocations for students and faculty members from less-developed districts across Balochistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and Gilgit-Baltistan to bridge regional academic disparities.

Application processing will be conducted exclusively via the unified HEC online portal, with selection determined by competitive standardized evaluations.
